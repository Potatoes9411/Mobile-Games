# Rooftop Run - blind round 1 (ours = A, reference wins, clear)
Reference wins composition, rendering, palette, characters, effects, readability (slightly), polish; HUD tie. "A reads as a tech demo next to it."
Gaps (ours):
1. Flat lighting: no gradients, shadows or light direction. Target: key light #FFD9A0 from upper left, shadow tint #6A4C8C @30%, shadow strip under each train ~0.04 sw, 2px rim light on the runner.
2. Empty playfield: lower half bare sleepers in 3 of 4 frames. Target: >= 2 of 3 lanes occupied in the nearest 60% of track; trains ~0.30 sw wide; coin lines of 8+ over train roofs.
3. Runner has no character: featureless capsule + red helmet, same stiff pose. Target: runner ~0.22 sw, 3-tone cel shading, hair under cap, clothing, backpack with secondary motion, readable run cycle; hoverboard with glowing trail.
4. Repeated scenery: same pastel townhouses everywhere. Target: landmark prop every ~40 m, 4+ facade variants per world, overhead dressing (bunting, signs, lamps), >= 2 themed worlds with distinct palettes.
5. Camera: too much sky and receding rails. Target: horizon at 28-30% from top, runner's feet ~80%, FOV showing side-lane edges; steeper, higher camera so trains fill the frame.
6. Effects: Target: coin burst 8-12 particles #FFE45C spanning 0.08 sw, white specular on coins, speed streaks on boost, landing dust puffs.
Keep: game-over panel; centred X2 MULTIPLIER callout and magnet timer; visible guard and dog; bold striped barriers.
